Experience

NIT Trichy
Jun 2025 — Dec 2025
Computer Vision & Data Science Research Intern · Trichy, Tamil Nadu
  • Developed VICTOR — a real-time multi-camera violence detection framework with identity-aware tracking, cross-camera person Re-ID, and aggressor attribution.
  • Built a feature extraction pipeline over RWF-2000, generating 5,292 temporal sequences with 541-dimensional multimodal behavioral representations.
